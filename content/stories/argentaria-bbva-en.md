title: The public-bank family inside BBVA
---

BBVA is not only Bilbao plus Vizcaya. Argentaria brought Banco Hipotecario de España, Banco Exterior, Caja Postal and the specialized public credit banks into the 1999 combination.

Those institutions were a public-bank complex, not a caja SIP. Treating 1999 as a simple private-bank merger erases that lineage.

BBV and Argentaria combined in 1999 to create present-day BBVA. The later Unnim and Catalunya Banc acquisitions sit on top of this older tree. Open Argentaria in 1999.
