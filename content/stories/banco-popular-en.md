title: What happened to Banco Popular
---

On 6 June 2017 the ECB judged Banco Popular failing or likely to fail. On 7 June the Single Resolution Board transferred the bank to Banco Santander for €1 after writing down and converting capital instruments. That is a resolution sale, not a negotiated merger.

Santander legally absorbed Popular only on 28 September 2018. Banesto shows the same split clock: Santander acquired it in 1994 and absorbed it in 2013. Banco Pastor had already entered Popular.

A map that stops in 2012 misses this. Open Banco Popular in 2017 to see the resolution edge. The later TSB purchase and the BPCE stake do not move Sabadell into Santander.
