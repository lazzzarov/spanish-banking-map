title: How seven cajas became Bankia
---

In 2010 seven savings banks — led by Caja Madrid and Bancaja, with La Caja de Canarias, Caixa Laietana, Caja de Ávila, Caja Segovia and Caja Rioja — entered a SIP whose central vehicle was Banco Financiero y de Ahorros (BFA). That is a systems-integration step, not a quiet rebrand and not yet Bankia.

In 2011 the cajas segregated banking business into BFA, and BFA then transferred the operating bank to Altae Banco, renamed Bankia. The familiar arrow Caja Madrid → Bankia hides those two corporate layers. BORME notices document the segregations separately.

Bankia later absorbed BMN (2018) and was itself absorbed by CaixaBank on 26 March 2021. Open Caja Madrid in 2010 to see the seven-node SIP before the BFA and Bankia steps.
