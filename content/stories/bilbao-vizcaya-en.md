title: How Bilbao and Vizcaya set the modern merger template
---

Banco de Bilbao was founded in 1857 and Banco de Vizcaya in 1901. Their boards agreed a combination in 1988; shareholders approved Banco Bilbao Vizcaya that June. It is the start of Spain’s modern large-bank merger era.

The deal anticipated Single Market scale economics. It is ordinary M&A among commercial banks, not crisis resolution.

BBV later combined with Argentaria in 1999. Open Banco de Bilbao in 1988 to see the first edge before BBVA exists.
