title: Spain closed more than half its bank branches
---

Banco de España records 45,662 branches at end-2008, 27,706 in 2017 and 23,340 in June 2020. Funcas later compiles 17,603 in 2023. Those points are selected observations, not one homogeneous series.

In 2017 some 4,109 municipalities had no branch, about 1.25 million people. Later inclusion work found that alternative channels, including mobile branches, improved some in-person coverage even as the fixed network shrank.

Consolidation and digitization moved together. They are not the same as market share. Open the figures page for definitions, then return to a caja on the map.
