title: Caja3’s two-stage arrival at Ibercaja
---

CAI, Caja Círculo de Burgos and Caja Badajoz formed Banco Grupo Caja3. Ibercaja Banco acquired 100% of that bank on 25 July 2013. The former cajas became Ibercaja shareholders at that stage.

Legal absorption followed in 2014. Acquisition and merger are not the same clock. The Zaragoza caja itself dates to 1876 and transferred banking business into Ibercaja Banco in 2011.

Open Caja3 in 2013 to see the share purchase before the 2014 absorption.
