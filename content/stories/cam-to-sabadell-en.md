title: CAM, from a failed SIP to Sabadell
---

CAM joined an approved 2010 SIP with Cajastur, Caja Cantabria and Caja de Extremadura. That project broke up. The other three later formed Liberbank; CAM did not go with them.

On 22 July 2011 Banco de España intervened CAM. Banking operations moved into Banco CAM, which FROB then sold. Sabadell acquired Banco CAM and absorbed it in 2012.

The same institution can sit on a failed path and a completed path. Open CAM in 2011 to see the intervention before the Sabadell sale.
