title: Two Catalan rescue routes into BBVA
---

Manlleu, Sabadell and Terrassa produced Unnim / Unnim Banc. Catalunya, Manresa and Tarragona produced CatalunyaCaixa and then Catalunya Banc. Same destination, different restructuring economics.

BBVA bought 100% of Unnim Banc for €1 in 2012 and registered the legal absorption in May 2013. It acquired about 98.4% of Catalunya Banc on 24 April 2015 and absorbed that bank in 2016.

Keeping Catalunya Banc as its own node preserves the FROB/privatization phase. Open Catalunya Banc in 2015 to see the acquisition before absorption.
