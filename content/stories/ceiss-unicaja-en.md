title: The long road from España-Duero to Unicaja
---

Caja España and Caja Duero merged in 2010 into Caja España-Duero. The caja then transferred financial activity to Banco CEISS / EspañaDuero. That is not yet Unicaja.

Unicaja legally absorbed Banco CEISS in September 2018, about eight years after the original caja combination. A 2014 control shorthand would flatten a clock the filings keep separate.

Unicaja itself comes from the 1991 combination of the Ronda, Cádiz, Almería, Málaga and Antequera cajas, plus Caja Jaén in 2010, with Liberbank absorbed on 30 July 2021. Open CEISS in 2018.
