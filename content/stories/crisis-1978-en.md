title: The banking crisis before 2008
---

Spain suffered a severe banking crisis from 1978 to 1985. Institutions such as Banca Catalana, Urquijo, Bankunión, Atlántico, Garriga Nogués and Coca disappeared as independents or moved into larger groups.

By the end of 1985 Banesto, Santander, Hispano Americano and Bilbao together represented about 41% of private-bank deposits. The later caja boom grew on top of an already concentrated private-bank core.
