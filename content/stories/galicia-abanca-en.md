title: How Galicia’s two cajas became ABANCA
---

Caixa Galicia and Caixanova merged in 2010 into Novacaixagalicia. The caja then transferred banking activity into NCG Banco. That bank was later privatized to the Banesco / Etcheverría group.

NCG Banco absorbed Banco Etcheverría in December 2014. The surviving legal bank took the ABANCA name. “Etcheverría became NCG” is the wrong direction.

FROB/FGD sold an 88.33% holding for about €1.003 billion. Open Novacaixagalicia in 2011 to see the transfer into NCG Banco.
