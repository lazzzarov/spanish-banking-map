title: The 1921 Banking Law and a concentrating system
---

The Ley de Ordenación Bancaria of 29 December 1921, associated with Francesc Cambó, reorganized the banking framework and Banco de España’s role. It is the century-scale starting point of this map.

The system was already concentrating. Banco de España historical work puts the six large banks’ deposit share at about 47.7% in 1922 and 64.5% in 1934. That is a historical-category statistic, not today’s credit-institution count.

A law is not an edge between two banks. Open the context layer in 1921, then jump to Bilbao–Vizcaya in 1988 to see when large-bank mergers become the main story.
