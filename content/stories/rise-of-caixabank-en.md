title: The rise of CaixaBank
---

CaixaBank is not the same legal person as la Caixa. The Caja de Pensiones was founded in 1904; the 1990 caja milestone and the 2011 banking-business transfer created the listed bank. Caixa Girona entered that caja line before the transfer.

Several 2010 vehicles later arrived at the same surviving bank: Banca Cívica (absorbed 2012), Banco de Valencia after FROB intervention, and Bankia’s SIP plus BMN in 2021. The Bankia legal absorption on 26 March 2021 is the last step on that path.

The merged group then reported €623.8 billion in assets and nearly 20 million customers. That is a sourced company figure from 2021, not a live metric on this map. Open CaixaBank in 2021 for the full tree.
