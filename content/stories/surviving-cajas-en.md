title: The two cajas that did not disappear
---

Spain entered the crisis with 45 savings banks. After the restructuring Banco de España identifies only two that remained in the traditional form: Caixa Ontinyent and Colonya–Caixa Pollença.

They are the counterexample to any line that says every caja vanished. Most others bankized, transferred business, or were absorbed. These two still take deposits as cajas.

Use the surviving-cajas filter on the map. This file is a genealogy of the eight large families plus these two exceptions, not a claim that nothing else survived in another legal form.
