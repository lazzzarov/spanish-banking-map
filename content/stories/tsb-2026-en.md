On this map the edge is a divestment / outbound sale. It is excluded from Spanish-parent lineage. Sabadell remains its own featured group, as it does after BPCE’s 7% stake.

Open TSB in 2026, then Sabadell. If Sabadell had moved under Santander, the 2025 failed bid would have been unnecessary.
